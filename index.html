Keep the sideline in sync

Score, rotation, and match log in one place.

Follow the current server and rotation, record substitutions and libero changes, manage timeouts, and keep a rally-by-rally activity history. If something was entered incorrectly, undo it, correct the score, or edit the match log.

Explore live scoring →

Capture the rally by tap or voice

Track more without staring at the screen.

Use focused match controls or speak a short rally description. VolleyTrack turns the transcript into suggested actions and lets you review them before they enter the match record. Voice interpretation needs a connection; ordinary scoring remains available locally.

How voice entry works →

Turn match activity into useful answers

See more than the final score.

Review serving, attacking, receiving, side-out, point-scoring, hitting-efficiency, and player contribution data. Create match, event, and season reports, then use AI-assisted analysis as a starting point for coaching review and shareable summaries.

VolleyTrack supports coach judgment; it does not replace it.

For family and friends

Give fans the match—not the control room.

Share a four-character code or QR code so family and friends can follow the live score, set, server, rotation, recent activity, and match summary in VolleyTrack Spectator View. Fans can cheer, react, and join Fan Zone while the scorer stays in control of the match record.

Fans can join the same VolleyTrack Spectator View from VolleyTrack or VolleyTally, the lighter scoreboard app from HarpElle.
