For coaches, stat keepers, and directors

A control room that still lets you coach.

VolleyTrack is designed for the people responsible for what happens next—not just what happened last. Keep the match state visible, capture the detail you value, and come back to a usable record after the final point.

The first-match promise

You do not have to learn everything before first serve.

Begin with a short practice set. Use Just Score until the core controls feel natural. Then add the roster, lineup, and rally actions that answer your coaching questions. The app's deeper tools are available when you are ready; they do not have to be part of every match.

The match-day workflow.

  1. 01

    Prepare

    Choose the team, rules, roster, and scoring depth.

  2. 02

    Run

    Score, rotate, substitute, correct, and monitor match state.

  3. 03

    Review

    Confirm the result and match log.

  4. 04

    Learn

    Read the team/player stats and generate reports.

  5. 05

    Share

    Send a spectator code during the match or a summary afterward.

For managers and directors

Build continuity beyond one scorer and one match.

Organize teams, seasons, events, and scheduled matches; keep rosters available; review results across a tournament or season; and sync data through an account when the workflow spans devices.

Trust statement

Assistance where it helps. Human judgment where it matters.

Voice entry is reviewed before it changes the match. AI-assisted summaries help organize and communicate what was recorded. Coaches and authorized team staff remain responsible for the accuracy and use of the match record.
