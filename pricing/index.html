Pricing FAQ

Does voice count every phrase?

No. The free allowance is counted by matches in which voice is enabled, not by each voice entry.

Can I score without Pro?

Yes. Core scoring is available on the Free plan.

Does deleting my VolleyTrack account cancel a subscription?

Not necessarily. Store subscriptions are managed separately through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel the subscription in the store account as well. See account deletion.
