VolleyTrack Spectator View

Follow the match without carrying the clipboard.

Enter the four-character code from your coach or scorer to see the live match story: score, sets, server, rotation, recent activity, and summaries. Cheer for your players and join the Fan Zone without changing the scorer's match record.

How to watch a live match How coaches share a match

What spectators can see

  • Current score and set progress.
  • Which team is serving.
  • Current rotation and shared roster/player names.
  • Recent scoring and match activity.
  • Timeouts and substitutions recorded by the scorer.
  • Between-set and final summaries when available.

Spectator View reflects information entered by the scorer. Network conditions and scoring corrections can change or delay what appears.

What spectators can do

  • Choose players to cheer for.
  • Send cheers and reactions.
  • Post in Fan Zone.
  • Ask the scorer to check the score or send an emergency alert.
  • Save a watched match for easier re-entry while it remains available.

Alerts are requests for the scorer's attention. They do not change the match automatically.

Privacy and publicness

Know what is shared before you join.

The match code gives access to the live match information the scorer shares, which may include team and player names. Your cheering choices stay private. If you post in Fan Zone, your chosen display name and message are visible to people watching that match. Do not share a match code more broadly than the team intends.

Availability

Live while the scorer shares it.

Spectator View needs an internet connection. Match access is temporary and may end after the live/post-match window or when the scorer stops sharing. If a code is not working, confirm the four characters and ask whether the match is still being shared.

VolleyTrack + VolleyTally

One live match. Two ways to follow.

Coaches run the match in VolleyTrack. Family and friends can join the same VolleyTrack Spectator View from VolleyTrack or VolleyTally. VolleyTally keeps the entry experience lighter for people who want the score and match activity without VolleyTrack's coaching workflow.
